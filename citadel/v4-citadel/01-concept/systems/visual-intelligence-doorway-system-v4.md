# Visual Intelligence Doorway System

## Location

The Visual Intelligence Room door sits on the circular wall of the Great Hall.

## Doorway Composition

[ Left Alcove ] [ Center Door ] [ Right Alcove ]

## Left Alcove

Knowledge Vault.

Default:
- Decorative object
- Crystal lantern or reliquary

Hidden Information Mode:
- Vaulted Key appears

## Center Door

Permanent room identity.

Possible visual symbols:
- Bronze Eye
- Crystal Lens
- Mechanical Iris

## Right Alcove

Active subcategory selector.

The active subcategory is represented as a rotating holographic artifact.

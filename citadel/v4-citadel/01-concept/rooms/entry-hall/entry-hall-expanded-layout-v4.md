# Entry Hall Expanded Layout

## Core Role

The Entry Hall is the threshold room. It is the first interior room after the visitor enters from outside.

## Canonical Door Count

The Entry Hall has 4 doors.

## Current Working Compass Assignment

- North: Reserved / future passage
- East: Great Hall
- South: Citadel Exterior
- West: Reserved / future passage

## Why East Leads to Great Hall in v4

The Great Hall has a known project detail: the South Door returns to the Small Library.

To preserve that, v4 assigns the Entry Hall connection to the Great Hall's West Door instead of the Great Hall's South Door.

This is marked as a working assignment and may be revised if older source files show a different canonical map.

## Render Priority

A good Entry Hall render must make the four-door layout visible or understandable.

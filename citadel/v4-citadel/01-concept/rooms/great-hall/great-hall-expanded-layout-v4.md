# Great Hall Expanded Layout

## Core Shape

The Great Hall is circular or strongly radial.

## Doors

The Great Hall has 8 compass doors:

- North
- Northeast
- East
- Southeast
- South
- Southwest
- West
- Northwest

## Niches

The Great Hall has 16 niches around the circular wall.

Rule:
- 2 niches per door
- Each door owns its adjacent niches
- One niche on the clockwise/right side
- One niche on the counterclockwise/left side

## Center

The center stays open.

The floor center contains a Buddhist eight-spoked wheel floor sigil. This should read as a calm navigation/knowledge emblem, not clutter.

## Floor

The floor style should feel ancient Roman / cathedral-like:
- Marble
- Circular inlays
- Compass or wheel geometry
- Polished stone
- Optional plants around edges or niches

## Plants

Plants may soften the monumental stone architecture and make the Great Hall feel alive.

## Purpose of the Niches

The 16 niches can support:
- Room-specific artifacts
- Statues
- Decorative objects
- Hidden clues
- Station previews
- Future interactive objects
- Door-owned state indicators

# Rendering Style Guide

## Overall Style

Cinematic fantasy realism.

The Citadel should feel physically real:
- Believable stone
- Aged wood
- Brass and bronze
- Warm light
- Crystal and holographic effects where appropriate
- Clear architectural orientation

## Camera

Use camera directions explicitly.

Examples:
- Camera at center of Great Hall facing E toward Visual Intelligence Room door.
- Camera in Entry Hall facing E toward Great Hall door.
- Camera in Small Library facing N toward the Great Hall return door.

## Materials

- Ancient carved stone
- Cathedral-like marble floor
- Dark oak
- Brass
- Bronze
- Stained glass
- Crystal lenses
- Holographic blue-white light
- Warm gold lantern light

## Lighting

- Warm interior light for human spaces
- Blue-white holographic light for intelligence systems
- Gold glow for hidden knowledge / Vaulted Key
- Natural skylight for Great Hall where appropriate

## Avoid

- Flat UI screens unless embedded as magical/physical artifacts
- Generic sci-fi rooms
- Doors without compass/orientation context
- Cluttered centers in the Great Hall

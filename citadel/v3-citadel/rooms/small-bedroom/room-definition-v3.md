# Small Bedroom

## Purpose

The Small Bedroom represents personal quarters inside the Citadel. It makes the Citadel feel livable rather than only monumental.

## Location Type

Private living space.

## Layout

Compact, practical, warm, and human-scale.

## Key Features

- Small bed
- Wooden bedside table
- Warm lantern or lamp
- Chest or storage trunk
- Simple writing surface or shelf
- Stone or wood walls
- Possibly a small window
- Personal artifacts

## Mood

Safe, restful, private, grounded.

## Render Notes

This room should contrast with the Great Hall. It should feel like a place where a person could actually rest, think, and return to after exploring the Citadel.

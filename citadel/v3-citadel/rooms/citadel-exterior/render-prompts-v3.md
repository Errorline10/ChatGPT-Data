# Citadel Exterior Render Prompts

Close view of the main entrance to a mountain citadel, huge carved stone doors, ancient symbols of knowledge, brass details, lanterns glowing beside the doorway, misty mountain air, cinematic fantasy realism, detailed stonework, inviting but monumental.

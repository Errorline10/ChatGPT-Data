# Citadel Exterior

## Purpose

The exterior establishes the Citadel as a place of knowledge, memory, discovery, and personal world-building. It is the first visual impression of the entire project.

## Location Type

Exterior approach / establishing environment.

## Architecture

The Citadel sits high on a mountain or elevated rocky formation, possibly above clouds. The structure should feel ancient, monumental, and defensible, but also inviting as a place of learning.

## Key Features

- Mountain-top Citadel
- Stone bridge or approach path
- Clouds or mist below
- Massive exterior walls
- Tall towers or spires
- Main entrance visible in the distance
- Strong sense of vertical height and scale

## Mood

Epic, mysterious, safe, intelligent, and slightly magical.

## Render Notes

The exterior should be used for establishing shots, splash screens, and “arrival at the Citadel” moments.

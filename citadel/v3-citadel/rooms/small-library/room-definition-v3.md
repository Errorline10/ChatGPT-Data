# Small Library

## Purpose

The Small Library is an intimate research and reading room. It is smaller and more personal than a grand archive.

## Location Type

Knowledge room / personal research space.

## Layout

The room should feel enclosed, warm, and useful. It may be connected from the Great Hall.

## Key Features

- Floor-to-ceiling bookshelves
- Reading chair
- Writing desk
- Brass reading lamps
- Dark wood furniture
- Possible fireplace
- Globe, maps, or index drawers
- Arched window with mountain view

## Mood

Quiet, focused, comfortable, intelligent.

## Render Notes

The Small Library should look usable. It is not just decorative. It should invite reading, planning, writing, and research.

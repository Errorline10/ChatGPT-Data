# Entry Hall

## Purpose

The Entry Hall is the first interior space after entering the Citadel. It acts as the transition between the outside world and the deeper knowledge spaces.

## Core Architectural Detail

The Entry Hall has four doors.

This should be treated as a canonical layout feature.

## Layout

The room should feel formal, balanced, and symmetrical enough to orient the visitor. It is not yet the large hub; it is a threshold room that prepares the visitor for the Great Hall.

## Door Count

Total doors: 4

Suggested interpretation:
- Main exterior entrance door behind the visitor
- Forward door leading to the Great Hall
- Left-side door to a secondary or future space
- Right-side door to a secondary or future space

## Key Features

- Four doors
- Vaulted ceiling
- Stone floor
- Introductory banners or plaques
- Warm light from sconces or lanterns
- Possible central rug or inlaid compass design
- Visual sense of arrival

## Mood

Welcoming, ordered, ceremonial, calm.

## Render Notes

The Entry Hall should make the viewer understand that they have crossed from the real world into the Citadel. It should not be too cluttered. Its most important render requirement is that the four-door layout is visible or clearly implied.

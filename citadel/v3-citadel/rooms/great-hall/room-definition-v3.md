# Great Hall

## Purpose

The Great Hall is the central hub of the Citadel. It is where major rooms are accessed and where the Citadel's larger navigation language becomes visible.

## Core Architectural Detail

The Great Hall has:
- 8 doors
- 16 niches on the circular walls

These should be treated as canonical layout features.

## Layout

The Great Hall is circular or strongly radial. The visitor should be able to understand it as a hub room.

## Door Count

Total doors: 8

Suggested use:
- One door back toward the Entry Hall
- One door to the Visual Intelligence Room
- One door to the Small Library
- One door to the Small Bedroom
- Four additional doors reserved for future rooms

## Niche Count

Total niches: 16

The niches are placed around the circular walls. These can contain statues, artifacts, display cases, banners, symbolic indicators, or future interactive stations.

## Relationship Between Doors and Niches

A useful pattern is:
- 8 major doors
- 16 smaller niches
- 2 niches between each major doorway, or one niche on each side of a door

This creates a consistent architectural rhythm around the circular wall.

## Key Features

- Circular wall structure
- 8 major doors
- 16 wall niches
- High domed or vaulted ceiling
- Central floor emblem or compass
- Large skylight or overhead light source
- Doorway alcove systems for specialized rooms
- Strong sense of orientation and discovery

## Mood

Grand, organized, magical, intelligent, and expandable.

## Render Notes

The Great Hall must show that it is a hub. Renders should clearly communicate multiple doorways and niches. Avoid rendering it like a simple corridor or rectangular room.

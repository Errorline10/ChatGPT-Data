# Citadel World Map

## Primary Flow

The visitor approaches the Citadel from outside, crosses toward the main structure, enters through the Entry Hall, then proceeds into the Great Hall.

The Great Hall is the major hub. It contains the doorways to specialized rooms and visual alcove/niche systems around the circular wall.

## Current Known Route

Citadel Exterior
→ Entry Hall
→ Great Hall

## Great Hall Connections

The Great Hall currently connects to:
- Small Library
- Small Bedroom
- Visual Intelligence Room
- Additional future rooms represented by remaining doors

## Doorway Language

Major doors use the Citadel doorway system:

Left Alcove = hidden/deeper knowledge state  
Center Door = room identity  
Right Alcove = active specialization or selector

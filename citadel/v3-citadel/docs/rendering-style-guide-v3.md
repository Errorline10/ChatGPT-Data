# Rendering Style Guide

## Overall Visual Style

The Citadel should feel like a real, physical place rather than a generic fantasy menu. The preferred rendering style is cinematic fantasy realism with believable stone, wood, metal, glass, candlelight, and atmospheric depth.

## Materials

Common materials:
- Ancient carved stone
- Dark oak and aged wood
- Brass and bronze mechanical details
- Stained glass
- Crystal lenses
- Holographic light projections
- Ironwork and heavy hinges
- Polished stone floors

## Lighting

Preferred lighting:
- Warm interior lantern and candlelight
- Natural light from tall windows or skylights
- Blue-white holographic accents for intelligent systems
- Gold light for knowledge, discovery, and hidden-vault indicators

## Camera Rules

For canonical room renders:
- Use wide-angle view when documenting room layout
- Use eye-level perspective unless a special architectural render is needed
- Include major doors and orientation landmarks
- Avoid clutter that hides the room structure

For detail renders:
- Focus on one architectural element
- Show object scale clearly
- Make mechanisms readable and usable

## Render Prompt Structure

Every render prompt should include:
1. Room name
2. Camera direction
3. Room geometry
4. Major doors
5. Major niches/alcoves
6. Lighting
7. Materials
8. Functional objects
9. Mood
10. Style tags

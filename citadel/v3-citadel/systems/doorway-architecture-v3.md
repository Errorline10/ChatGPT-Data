# Doorway Architecture System

## Purpose

The doorway architecture system allows rooms in the Great Hall to communicate state without relying on flat menus.

## Standard Pattern

Left Alcove = Hidden Knowledge  
Center Door = Room Identity  
Right Alcove = Active Specialization

## Left Alcove: Knowledge Vault

The left alcove may look decorative at first. It becomes meaningful when hidden or deeper information is available.

Default:
- Decorative artifact
- Crystal lantern
- Sealed reliquary
- Ornate display case

Hidden Information Mode:
- Vaulted Key appears
- Indicates hidden metadata, source references, OCR layers, confidence analysis, annotations, or deeper context

## Center Door: Room Identity

The center door remains stable. It visually identifies the room even when subcategories or modes change.

## Right Alcove: Active Specialization

The right alcove shows the active subcategory, mode, or station. It can use a holographic object rather than a text menu.

## Reusable Citadel Rule

This pattern may be reused across multiple Great Hall doors:

Left Alcove = hidden/deeper knowledge  
Center Door = room identity  
Right Alcove = active specialization
